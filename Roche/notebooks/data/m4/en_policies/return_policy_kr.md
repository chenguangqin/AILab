# Return Policy (Republic of Korea)

## 1. Statutory Withdrawal Period

Pursuant to Article 17 of the Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce (전자상거래법) of the Republic of Korea, consumers shall be entitled to withdraw from a distance-selling contract within **seven (7) calendar days** from the date of receipt of the goods. The seller shall not impose any penalty fee for withdrawal during this period.

## 2. Condition Requirements

The right of withdrawal shall be preserved provided that:

(a) the original packaging remains substantially intact;
(b) the product has not been used in a manner inconsistent with normal pre-purchase inspection;
(c) the value of the goods has not been materially diminished by the consumer's actions; and
(d) the serial number on the device matches the seller's records.

## 3. Devices and SIM Activation

Mobile devices sold through Korean carriers (SK Telecom, KT, LG U+) — including Galaxy S24 Ultra (KR SKU) and iPhone 15 Pro Max (KR SKU) — that have been activated against a Korean carrier's network may be subject to a USIM activation fee deduction of up to KRW 35,200 from the refund.

## 4. Return Shipping

The customer shall pay return shipping costs (typically KRW 5,000–10,000 via CJ Logistics or Hanjin Express) when exercising voluntary withdrawal. The seller shall pay return shipping costs only in cases of verified defect, mis-shipment, or damage in transit.

## 5. Refund Schedule

Refunds shall be issued within three (3) business days of receipt and verification of the returned goods, in accordance with Article 18(2) of the aforementioned Act. Should the seller fail to meet this deadline, statutory delay interest of fifteen percent (15%) per annum shall accrue.

## 6. Value-Added Tax

The 10% Korean VAT (부가가치세) shall be refunded together with the principal. For business-to-business (B2B) transactions, customers shall provide the original tax invoice (세금계산서) at the time of return for proper VAT credit reversal.

## 7. Non-Returnable Items

Hygiene-sensitive audio products (e.g., Galaxy Buds3 Pro, AirPods Pro 2) with broken seals shall not be eligible for return. Custom-configured laptops (e.g., MacBook Pro M3 BTO units, ThinkPad X1 Carbon CTO) ordered to bespoke specifications are non-returnable except in cases of manufacturing defect.
