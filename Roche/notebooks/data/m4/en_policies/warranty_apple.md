# Apple Limited Warranty (Resale Channel Summary)

## 1. Coverage Period

Apple, Inc. ("Apple") warrants its branded hardware products — including, without limitation, iPhone 15 Pro Max, MacBook Pro (M3 series), iPad Pro, Apple Watch, AirPods Pro 2, and HomePod — against defects in materials and workmanship for a period of **one (1) year** from the date of original retail purchase by the end-user purchaser ("Warranty Period").

## 2. Geographic Scope

The Apple Limited Warranty for iPhone is generally region-locked: handsets purchased in Mainland China, Hong Kong SAR, Japan, the United States, and the European Union are warrantied within their respective region of purchase. iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch products carry a worldwide limited warranty subject to local availability of authorized service providers.

## 3. AppleCare+ Extended Coverage

End-users may elect to purchase AppleCare+ within sixty (60) days of original retail purchase, which extends warranty coverage to **two (2) or three (3) years** depending on product category, and adds accidental damage protection (ADP) subject to per-incident service fees:

(a) iPhone 15 Pro Max screen damage: USD 29 per incident;
(b) iPhone 15 Pro Max other accidental damage: USD 99 per incident;
(c) MacBook Pro 14" / 16" screen or external enclosure damage: USD 99 per incident;
(d) MacBook Pro other accidental damage: USD 299 per incident;
(e) AirPods Pro 2 accidental damage: USD 29 per incident.

## 4. Exclusions

The Apple Limited Warranty does not cover: (a) damage caused by accident, abuse, misuse, liquid contact (except where IP rating applies and is not breached), or unauthorized modification; (b) cosmetic damage including scratches and dents; (c) consumable parts including batteries that have been depleted through normal usage (separate battery service program applies); (d) products with the serial number altered, defaced, or removed; (e) software issues caused by third-party applications.

## 5. Service Channels

Authorized service may be obtained through: (a) Apple Retail Stores, (b) Apple Authorized Service Providers (AASP), or (c) Apple's official online support portal at support.apple.com. Mail-in repair turnaround is typically five (5) to seven (7) business days; in-store walk-in repairs may be completed same-day for select models.

## 6. Statutory Rights Preservation

Nothing in this Apple Limited Warranty shall affect any statutory rights of the consumer under applicable law (including but not limited to the EU Consumer Sales Directive 1999/44/EC, the US Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act, and the Japan Product Liability Act). Where conflicts arise, statutory rights shall prevail to the extent required.
