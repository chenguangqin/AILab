# Samsung Limited Warranty (Resale Channel Summary)

## 1. Coverage Period

Samsung Electronics Co., Ltd. ("Samsung") warrants its branded mobile and consumer electronics products — including, without limitation, Galaxy S24 Ultra, Galaxy Z Fold 5, Galaxy Tab S9 series, Galaxy Watch 6, and Galaxy Buds3 Pro — against defects in materials and workmanship for a period of **one (1) year** from the original retail purchase date in the country of sale.

## 2. Battery and Charger Coverage

Lithium-ion batteries and bundled chargers are covered for a separate period of **six (6) months** from original retail purchase. Battery degradation below eighty percent (80%) of rated capacity within the coverage period qualifies for a replacement battery at no charge under most regional Samsung warranty programs.

## 3. Samsung Care+ Extended Protection

Customers may purchase Samsung Care+ within thirty (30) days of original purchase to extend warranty coverage to **two (2) years** with accidental damage protection. Service fees per incident are as follows (USD equivalents shown for reference):

(a) Galaxy S24 Ultra screen replacement: USD 99 per incident;
(b) Galaxy S24 Ultra other accidental damage: USD 199 per incident;
(c) Galaxy Tab S9 Ultra screen damage: USD 149 per incident;
(d) Galaxy Buds3 Pro accidental damage: USD 39 per incident;
(e) Theft and loss coverage (select markets): USD 249 per incident.

## 4. Region of Purchase Limitations

Samsung mobile devices sold in Korea (KR SKU), Japan (JP SKU), Mainland China (CN SKU), and Latin America (LATAM SKU) carry warranty validity primarily within their region of original sale. Cross-region service may be available at the discretion of the local Samsung Service Center but is not guaranteed and may incur diagnostic fees of up to USD 50.

## 5. Exclusions

The Samsung Limited Warranty does not cover: (a) damage from impact, drop, or improper use; (b) liquid damage in excess of the device's IP rating; (c) damage from non-Samsung-certified accessories or unauthorized firmware modifications (including bootloader unlock); (d) cosmetic wear; (e) consumable wear items.

## 6. Service Channels

Warranty service may be initiated through Samsung Members app, Samsung official website (samsung.com), Samsung Experience Stores, or Samsung Authorized Service Centers. Door-to-door pickup service is available in select metropolitan areas including Seoul, Tokyo, Shanghai, Singapore, London, and New York at no additional cost.

## 7. Knox Security Notice

Devices with the Knox Warranty Bit triggered (typically by rooting, custom recovery, or unauthorized OS modifications) shall void hardware warranty for software-related claims. Hardware-only defect claims may still be honored at Samsung's sole discretion.
