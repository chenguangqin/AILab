# Return Policy (United States)

## 1. Eligibility Window

Customers residing in the United States may return eligible products purchased through this store within **fourteen (14) calendar days** of the original delivery date as recorded by the carrier. Returns initiated after the fourteenth (14th) day shall not be accepted, except where required by applicable federal or state consumer protection statutes (e.g., Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act).

## 2. Condition Requirements

To qualify for a refund, the returned merchandise must be:

(a) in its original, unused, and unmodified condition;
(b) accompanied by all original packaging, accessories, manuals, and proof-of-purchase documentation;
(c) free from physical damage, liquid intrusion, or evidence of tampering; and
(d) not activated against any cellular network or paired with any third-party account in the case of mobile devices (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro Max, Galaxy S24 Ultra).

## 3. Restocking Fees

A restocking fee of fifteen percent (15%) of the original purchase price shall apply to opened-box returns of the following categories: laptops (e.g., MacBook Pro M3, ThinkPad X1 Carbon), tablets, drones (e.g., DJI Mini 4 Pro), and audio equipment exceeding USD 500 in value (e.g., Sony WH-1000XM5, Bose QuietComfort Ultra). Sealed returns are exempt from restocking fees.

## 4. Return Shipping

The customer shall bear the cost of return shipping unless the return is necessitated by a verified defect, manufacturing fault, or shipment error attributable to the seller. In such cases, the seller shall provide a prepaid USPS or UPS Ground label valued up to USD 30.

## 5. Refund Disbursement

Approved refunds shall be issued to the original method of payment within seven (7) to ten (10) business days following inspection of the returned goods at our New Jersey fulfillment center. Refunds for credit card purchases may take an additional one (1) to two (2) billing cycles to appear, subject to the issuing bank's processing schedule.

## 6. Non-Returnable Items

The following items are explicitly non-returnable: opened in-ear or over-ear headphones (e.g., AirPods Pro 2, Galaxy Buds3 Pro), digital download codes, gift cards, custom-engraved products, and items marked "Final Sale" at checkout.

## 7. Dispute Resolution

Any disputes arising from returns shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-law principles. Customers may also pursue remedies through the Better Business Bureau (BBB) or applicable state attorney general's office.
