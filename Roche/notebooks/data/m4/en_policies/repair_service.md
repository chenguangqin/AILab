# Repair Service Policy

## 1. Service Channels

Customers seeking post-purchase repair service for products procured through this store may choose from the following service channels, each with distinct turnaround and cost characteristics:

(a) **Authorized Service Provider (ASP) network**: Recommended for warranty-covered repairs; uses original equipment manufacturer (OEM) parts and preserves warranty status. Examples include Apple Authorized Service Providers for iPhone 15 Pro Max and MacBook Pro M3, and Samsung Service Centers for Galaxy S24 Ultra and Galaxy Tab S9.
(b) **Mail-in Service**: Available globally via prepaid carrier label. Diagnostic fee of USD 35 applies if no fault is found or if the issue is determined to be out-of-warranty.
(c) **Walk-in Service**: Available at our flagship service centers in Shanghai, Tokyo (Shibuya), Seoul (Gangnam), and Singapore. Same-day repair for select issues subject to parts availability.

## 2. Diagnostic and Estimate Process

Upon receipt of a device, technicians shall conduct a non-destructive diagnostic assessment within three (3) business days and issue a written repair estimate listing:

(a) the diagnosed root cause;
(b) the proposed repair scope;
(c) parts and labor costs;
(d) estimated turnaround time.

Customers must approve the estimate in writing before any chargeable work commences. Refusal to approve incurs a USD 35 diagnostic fee plus return shipping.

## 3. Out-of-Warranty Repair Pricing (Reference)

The following indicative prices apply for out-of-warranty mail-in repairs at our service centers. Prices are subject to change and final invoiced amount may vary:

(a) iPhone 15 Pro Max screen replacement (OEM): approximately USD 379;
(b) iPhone 15 Pro Max battery replacement: approximately USD 99;
(c) MacBook Pro 14" M3 battery replacement: approximately USD 199;
(d) MacBook Pro 16" M3 logic board repair: starts at USD 599;
(e) Galaxy S24 Ultra screen replacement: approximately USD 269;
(f) Sony WH-1000XM5 driver replacement: approximately USD 89;
(g) DJI Mini 4 Pro propeller and gimbal alignment: approximately USD 149.

## 4. Repair Warranty

All repairs performed by our service centers carry a **ninety (90) day** repair warranty covering the specific component repaired or replaced. This repair warranty does not extend the original product warranty period nor does it cover unrelated faults.

## 5. Data Liability

Customers are strongly advised to back up all data prior to submitting devices for service. The seller shall not be liable for loss, corruption, or unauthorized disclosure of customer data during the repair process. Devices submitted with screen-locks active may be subject to additional handling delays or technician-side bypass charges.

## 6. Loaner Device Program

For premium members (Gold tier and above) and AppleCare+ / Samsung Care+ subscribers, complimentary loaner devices may be available during the repair window, subject to inventory and a refundable security deposit equivalent to fifty percent (50%) of the loaner's MSRP.
