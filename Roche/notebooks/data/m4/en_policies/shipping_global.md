# Global Shipping Policy

## 1. Scope and Carriers

This Global Shipping Policy ("Policy") governs the international fulfillment of orders dispatched from our bonded warehouses in Hong Kong SAR, Tokyo, Seoul, Frankfurt, and Newark. Shipments shall be tendered to one of the following carriers based on origin, destination, and declared value: DHL Express, FedEx International Priority, UPS Worldwide Saver, SF International, or Japan Post EMS.

## 2. Transit Times

Standard transit times, exclusive of customs clearance delays, shall be as follows:

(a) East Asia (Mainland China, Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan): three (3) to five (5) business days;
(b) Southeast Asia (Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia, Philippines): five (5) to eight (8) business days;
(c) United States (continental): seven (7) to ten (10) business days; Alaska and Hawaii add three (3) days;
(d) European Union and United Kingdom: eight (8) to twelve (12) business days;
(e) Australia and New Zealand: seven (7) to ten (10) business days;
(f) Latin America, Middle East, Africa: ten (10) to twenty (20) business days, subject to local customs throughput.

## 3. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges are calculated based on dimensional weight (DIM weight, divisor 5000 cm³/kg) or actual weight, whichever is greater. Heavy items such as MacBook Pro M3 16" or DJI Mini 4 Pro Fly More Combo are typically billed by actual weight; small accessories such as USB-C cables or Anker PowerCore are billed by DIM weight.

## 4. Customs Duties and Import Taxes

All cross-border shipments are dispatched on a Delivered Duty Unpaid (DDU) basis unless explicitly marked as DDP at checkout. The recipient shall bear any applicable import duties, value-added taxes, processing fees, and brokerage charges levied by the destination country's customs authority. Refusal to pay such charges shall result in the package being returned to origin at the customer's expense.

## 5. Restricted Items

Lithium-ion battery products (e.g., Anker PowerCore, drone batteries for DJI Mini 4 Pro) are subject to IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations and may not be airfreighted to certain destinations including but not limited to Russia, Belarus, and Cuba. Customers shall verify destination eligibility prior to purchase.

## 6. Liability Limitation

Carrier liability is limited to USD 100 per shipment unless declared value insurance is purchased at checkout. For shipments containing iPhone 15 Pro Max, MacBook Pro M3, or other items exceeding USD 1,000, declared value insurance is strongly recommended at a premium of one percent (1%) of declared value.
