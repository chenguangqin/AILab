# Return Policy (Japan)

## 1. Statutory Cooling-Off Period

In accordance with the Act on Specified Commercial Transactions (特定商取引法) of Japan, customers who have purchased goods through mail-order channels shall have the right to cancel and return their order within **eight (8) calendar days** of receipt, provided that the merchandise has not been opened or used. Cooling-off does not apply to in-store pickup transactions.

## 2. Condition Requirements

To be eligible for return, the product must satisfy all of the following:

(a) original factory seal remains intact;
(b) all accessories, including warranty cards, manuals, and chargers, are present;
(c) the outer shipping carton is undamaged; and
(d) the serial number matches the original invoice.

## 3. Devices Sold via Carrier Lock

Mobile handsets sold through Japanese carriers (NTT Docomo, KDDI au, SoftBank, Rakuten Mobile) — including iPhone 15 Pro Max (Japan SKU) and Galaxy S24 Ultra (Japan SKU) — are subject to additional carrier-imposed conditions. Returns must be processed within **seven (7) calendar days** at the original point of sale, and any SIM card activation may render the device non-returnable.

## 4. Return Shipping

Within Japan, returns must be sent via Yamato Transport (ヤマト運輸) or Japan Post (日本郵便) using a tracked service. The seller shall bear return shipping costs only when the product is verified as defective by our Yokohama service center. Otherwise, return shipping is borne by the customer at a flat rate of JPY 800–1,500 depending on prefecture.

## 5. Refund Method

Refunds shall be processed within ten (10) business days of receipt and inspection. Refund methods supported include credit card (Visa, JCB, Mastercard, AMEX), bank transfer (国内銀行振込), and convenience store payment reversal (コンビニ決済). Cash-on-delivery (代金引換) refunds are issued via bank transfer only.

## 6. Consumption Tax

The 10% Japanese Consumption Tax (消費税) shall be refunded together with the principal amount. For inbound cross-border returns, customers should consult their local customs authority regarding any duty refund eligibility.

## 7. Non-Returnable Items

Sealed audio equipment (e.g., Sony WH-1000XM5, Bose QuietComfort Ultra) becomes non-returnable once the hygienic seal is broken. Drones (e.g., DJI Mini 4 Pro) registered to the operator's MyDJI account are non-returnable.
